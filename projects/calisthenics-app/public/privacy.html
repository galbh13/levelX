levelx — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

levelx is a calisthenics training app used by clients of a personal coach. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and who can see it. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use any advertising or analytics trackers.

Who runs this app

levelx is operated by Gal Ben Hamo. For any privacy question or request, contact the.handstand.system@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address and a securely hashed password, used only to sign you in.
  • Profile information — display name, optional profile picture, and your training class, level, and progression.
  • Training data — workouts, exercises, quest and daily-quest completions, scheduled sessions, and progress history you record in the app.
  • Check-in media — photos and videos you choose to upload to show your form or progress. These are uploaded only when you explicitly select them.
  • Messages — messages you send to your coach and to community groups inside the app.

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing activity, device identifiers for advertising, or any data for profiling or marketing.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos and media — requested only when you tap to upload a video or profile picture, so you can pick a file. The app does not browse or read your library in the background.

Why we use it

Your data is used solely to run the app and deliver coaching: to authenticate you, show your training programme and progress, let your coach review your form and adjust your plan, and enable community features you opt into. It is not used for advertising or sold to anyone.

Who can see your data

  • You — full access to your own profile, training data, and uploads.
  • Your coach (admin) — can view your profile, training history, and check-in media in order to coach you.
  • Other members — only content you deliberately post to a community group or shared challenge is visible to that group's members.

Service providers

We use a small number of processors purely to host and deliver the service:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage.
  • Cloudinary — video hosting and format conversion for uploaded videos.
  • Expo (EAS) — app hosting and delivery of app updates.

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. Data may be stored on servers outside your country, protected by the providers' standard safeguards.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. If you stop training with us, you may ask for your account and its contents to be deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete your account and its contents, or export your data. Email the.handstand.system@gmail.com and we will act on the request within 30 days.

Security

Access is protected by password authentication and database row-level security rules that prevent one user from reading another user's private data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children

levelx is not intended for children under 13. If you are under 18, you should only use the app with the consent of a parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and notify users in the app where appropriate.
