## Full description (max 4000 chars)
levelx is a calisthenics training app built for clients of a personal coach.
It turns your training into a clear progression you can actually see: a class
and level you advance through, quests that unlock as you get stronger, and a
weekly schedule your coach builds around you.

WHAT YOU GET

• Your own training plan — workouts and weekly schedule set up by your coach,
  ready every time you open the app.
• Quest progression — advance through classes and levels by completing real
  training milestones, not arbitrary points.
• Guided workout mode — go set by set with clear targets, and log what you did.
• Exercise gallery — see how each movement should look before you attempt it.
• Progress check-ins — upload a short video of your form and get feedback from
  your coach directly in the app.
• Community — train alongside other members with shared challenges and groups.

BUILT AROUND COACHING, NOT ALGORITHMS

levelx isn't a generic workout generator. Your class, your level and your
programme are set by a real coach who reviews your training and moves you
forward when you're ready. The app is the tool that carries it day to day.

FREE — NO ADS, NO IN-APP PURCHASES

The app is completely free to use, contains no advertising, and sells nothing.
There are no trackers and no analytics. Coaching is arranged with your coach
directly; the app simply gives you access to your training.

ACCESS

levelx is for existing coaching clients. You'll receive a login from your coach.
