# Communication Style

## Working with Gal
- Be efficient. No fluff, no filler.
- Tone: casual and direct.
- Gal has solid understanding — don't over-explain basics unless asked.
- When Gal hits something complex, break it down clearly and solve it step by step.

## App-Facing Content (for students)
- Tone: casual, warm, motivational
- Focus on student success and becoming their best version
- Keep copy simple and action-oriented
